Results

Ping pong

Your paddle follows your hand. Stand where you want your end of the table, facing Phone A, and tap Start match.

This phone is a camera

Games run on Phone A.

Align the cameras first, then tap Start match.

Match

Strong assist steers returns onto the table and uses a forgiving hit zone; Light can go long or wide; Off is pure physics. First to 11, win by 2.

Stats

Tracking runs ~100–200 ms behind; the paddle is drawn where your hand is predicted to be now.

Playhouse

Put your character on a stage. Give each character its own backdrop picture, then move — the character performs in front of it.

This phone is a camera

The playhouse runs on Phone A.

Choose a character, upload a backdrop, and step into view.

Backdrop

Character song

The song belongs to the character chosen above: it starts when you open the Playhouse with them (or switch to them) and loops. Switching character switches song. Uploaded songs stay on this device. On iPhone and iPad use the side buttons for volume.

3D characters (VRM)

Import rigged 3D avatars in VRM format — make your own in VRoid Studio (free) or download one from VRoid Hub. They join every character list and follow your body, head, hands and fingers, with moving hair and clothes.

Imported characters are kept on this device. Check each model's licence before using it in public. 3D characters show in the Playhouse and Drums; other views show the stick figure.

Stage



Move the floor line to where the floor meets the picture's back wall, so feet land on the ground. Snapshot saves backdrop + character as a PNG. Cycle characters switches to the next character — with its own backdrop — every 15 seconds.

360° panorama

The character stays in the middle while the camera slowly circles round them, so you see every side of the character and the whole panorama behind. Drag the picture to turn it yourself. Characters without a panorama use their backdrop, mirrored round into a circle.

Flanking characters

Backup dancers: one or two extra characters a step behind you, copying your moves.

Effects

Effects follow the chosen part and fade away; moving faster makes more of them. Right/left are the character's own right and left.

Theremin

Drums

Air drums: a drum kit appears around you. Hit the drums and cymbals from above with your hands, stamp your right foot for the bass drum and lift your left foot to open the hi-hat.

This phone is a camera

The drums run on Phone A.

Stand where you want to play, facing Phone A, and press Play drums. The kit appears around where you stand.

Play

Hit downwards, like a real drum: the sound plays as your hand passes down through the drum's surface, and the harder you hit, the louder it is. A glowing dot shows which drum your hand is over.

Metronome & recording

Record a beat, then play it back and drum along with yourself.

Control

Put Motion Lab on your TV, aim at it with your hand, drive it from the couch with gestures, and record a tuning session.

This phone is a camera

Control runs on Phone A.

1

TV display

Open this link in the TV's web browser (or a laptop/streaming stick on the TV). The TV shows whatever this screen is showing — 3D view, games, Playhouse, Theremin. Click once on the TV to allow sound.

2

Pointing at the TV

Calibration shows 5 targets on the TV — point and fire (thumb down) at each. Stand where you'll play; recalibrate if you move a lot.

3

Couch control

Hold up an open palm for a second (or wave) to open the menu on the TV. Point at a tile and pinch (or fire your finger gun) — or just hover over it. Make a fist with your other hand to close.

Gestures



Recognised: open palm 🖐, fist ✊, point ☝️, pinch 🤏, peace ✌️, thumbs up 👍, finger gun 👉 / fired 💥, hand raised, wave. Bars show each finger's straightness (green = straight). Needs Body + detailed hands.




Tuning session


A 90-second guided recording that lets the tracking be tuned to your room, phones and hands. Run it on Phone A with all phones connected, cameras on (Body + detailed hands) and aligned. A voice tells you what to do; at the end a motionlab-tuning-…json file downloads — send it over.
